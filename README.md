PhysiqueProgress is a smart fitness progress tracking app designed to help you visually measure and analyze your body transformation over time.

Instead of guessing your progress, PhysiqueProgress uses image analysis and body metrics to give you meaningful insights into posture, symmetry, physique development, and overall progress.

What PhysiqueProgress helps you do:

- Capture and organize physique progress photos
- Automatically analyze posture, symmetry, and physique metrics
- Track full-body and upper-body progress
- View your transformation history in one place
- See progress analytics and trends over time
- Create and follow daily fitness plans
- Use the home screen widget to quickly view your daily workout and meal plan
